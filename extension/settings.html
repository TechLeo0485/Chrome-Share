Connection settings

Most connections work directly, with no setup. On very strict networks (some mobile carriers, office/school firewalls, or across national firewalls) a direct connection can't be made and the video has to be relayed through a TURN server. There's no free public relay that works without an account, so this extension doesn't ship one — but you can add your own.

Speed limit

Caps how fast the shared tab's video and sound may flow — useful on metered or slow connections, or to leave bandwidth for other things. Applies whether you're sharing or viewing; if both of you set one, the lower limit wins. Takes effect immediately, even during a session.

Your TURN server (optional)

Several TURN providers offer a free tier with sign-up — search for "free TURN server". Copy the TURN URL(s), username and password they give you below. Only one of you (host or friend) needs to set this up.

Tips for a more stable session

  • Settings apply to the next share or join — restart an active one to use them.
  • Include a turn:…:443?transport=tcp or turns:…:443 URL if your provider offers one — those get through the most firewalls.
  • If the connection drops, the viewer reconnects on its own with the same code. Nobody needs to re-send anything.
  • On slow links, the picture lowers its frame rate first to keep text sharp — that's expected.
